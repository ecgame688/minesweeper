Privacy Policy

Minesweeper · Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Minesweeper ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The App is free and the core game plays fully offline.
  • We do not require you to create an account with your name or email, and we do not collect your contacts, photos, or location.
  • Optional online features (leaderboards, push reminders) use an anonymous guest identity only.
  • The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect device/advertising identifiers.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete my data.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences and progress (language, theme, difficulty, sound/haptics, statistics, daily streak, and a saved game to resume) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes them.

Online features (optional)

When online features are used, the App connects to our game backend to provide:

  • Anonymous guest account — a randomly generated device identifier is used to create a guest account. It is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Leaderboards — your best completion times and the difficulty played are submitted so your ranking can be shown.
  • Display name — an optional nickname you choose to appear on the leaderboard. Please do not enter personal information as your display name.

We do not sell this data and do not attach it to a personal profile.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to send occasional gameplay reminders. A device push token is generated and registered with our backend solely to deliver these messages. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in your device settings at any time.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may collect and process data such as device identifiers, advertising ID, and app usage to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In regions that require it (e.g., EEA/UK), the App requests your consent for personalized ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your data from Settings → Delete my data. This removes your local preferences and progress, and requests deletion of your anonymous guest account, leaderboard scores, and push token from our backend. You may also email us to request deletion.

Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are served in a family-friendly manner where required.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell your personal data. Advertising data is processed by Google AdMob, and push delivery by Firebase Cloud Messaging, as described above.

Your choices

  • Play fully offline — simply do not use online leaderboards.
  • Turn off notifications in Settings or device settings.
  • Limit ad personalization in device settings (Ads / Advertising ID).
  • Delete all your data from Settings → Delete my data.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
